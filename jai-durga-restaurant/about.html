Tradition & Purity

Crafting Happiness One Mithai & Meal at a Time

Situated at the bustling heart of Jamalpur (Bihar 811214), Jai Durga Sweet Shop & Restaurant started with a clear vision: to bring pure, unadulterated traditional Indian sweets and hearty family meals to the community at honest, affordable prices.

Over the years, we have grown into one of Jamalpur's most recognized food landmarks, with over 1,000+ positive customer reviews on Google. From early morning breakfast seekers enjoying fresh Dosas and hot Kachoris, to families gathering in the evening for rich Shahi Paneer, Mughlai Parathas, and Veg Hakka Noodles — Jai Durga is a household name for taste and trust.

"Purity is not a compromise for us; it is our foundation. Every batch of sweet is prepared using fresh milk and pure ghee."
