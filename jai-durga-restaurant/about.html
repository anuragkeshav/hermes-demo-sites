Discover the story behind Jamalpur's trusted dining haven and pure desi ghee sweets destination.

The Tradition of Taste

Built on pure ingredients and family warmth.

In the historic railway town of Jamalpur, Bihar, Jai Durga was founded with a singular focus: serving wholesome meals and authentic Indian sweets without taking shortcuts on ingredient purity.

Every day begins before sunrise with fresh farm milk deliveries. Our halwais start boiling milk for thick saffron Rasmalai and curdling chhena for sponge Rosogullas. By 7:00 AM, the breakfast tawa starts sizzling with butter masala dosas.
