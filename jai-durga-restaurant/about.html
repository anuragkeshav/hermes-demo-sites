The Jai Durga Tradition

A Journey of Flavour, Freshness & Family Values

Established with a humble mission to provide unadulterated pure desi ghee sweets and hygienic, appetizing meals to the citizens of Jamalpur and nearby areas, Jai Durga Sweet Shop & Restaurant has grown into a revered dining icon.

Every single morning starts before sunrise with boiling farm-fresh milk to make our signature chhena for sponge Rosogulla and rich Rasmalai. As the clock strikes 7:00 AM, our tawa starts sizzling with crispy South Indian Dosas, followed by afternoon royal thalis and evening Chinese and Mughlai feasts.
