Our Heritage

A Journey of Tradition, Freshness & Integrity

Founded with a steadfast commitment to uncompromised purity, Jai Durga Sweet Shop & Restaurant has served generations of families across Jamalpur and Munger with authentic sweets and wholesome meals.

Our confectioners begin before sunrise with farm-fresh milk to craft fresh chhena for sponge Rosogullas and Kesariya Rasmalai. By 7:00 AM, our breakfast grills begin serving golden crispy Dosas, leading into afternoon thalis and evening tandoor and Mughlai feasts.
